Corrections policy

DFRI corrects verified errors in public, versioned records. It never silently replaces a published estimate, prediction, grade, assumption, or source citation.

Report an error

Email {{ commercial_license_contact }}. Include the page or feed URL, company or prediction ID, period, disputed value or citation, and the evidence supporting the concern. Do not send borrower, account, loan-level, credential, or other personal information.

We aim to acknowledge a complete report within five business days. Acknowledgement is not a finding; verification may require checking the cited filing, first-print release, registry version, and deterministic replay.

How a correction publishes

  1. The report is reproduced against the exact published version and evidence set.
  2. A confirmed error receives a new versioned row or superseding record. Existing rows, prediction timestamps, grades, canonical hashes, and cited permalinks are not edited or deleted.
  3. The public changelog names what changed, why, the affected period or company, and the replacement version.
  4. Prior values remain retrievable through their immutable permalinks, versioned feeds, publication history, and changelog links.

If a report is not confirmed, the evidence and conclusion are returned to the reporter when contact information is available. Methodological disagreement without a factual error is documented as feedback rather than rewritten as a correction.

Urgent clock issues

A missing scheduled prediction, missed first-print grade, mutated ledger record, or broken immutable permalink is treated as an operational incident. The append-only record is preserved while the publication or automation path is repaired.
