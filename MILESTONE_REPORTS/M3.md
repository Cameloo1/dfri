## Published result and inputs

The public 2026-Q1 aggregate estimate is 3.97% at the midpoint, with a 3.66%–4.29% 10th/90th
percentile band. Its midpoint numerator is approximately $14.6 billion against an estimated
$368.0 billion covered-company U.S. consumer-revenue denominator. These are modeled estimates,
not observed company disclosures.

The observed quarterly inputs are complete first-print Board G.19 flows:

- Revolving credit: $15.4 billion across the January, February, and March 2026 target months.
- Nonrevolving credit: $27.0 billion across the same target months.
- Dated sources: [March 6](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20260306/),
  [April 7](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20260407/), and
  [May 7](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20260507/).

Q2 is not substituted into this first publication because the June target month's G.19 first
print was not yet available at the control timestamp. Revised data and an invented completion are
both prohibited.
